# Summary

Most people in Southampton notice problems where they live, but very few ever tell the council. Having your say usually means a long survey, so most people never do. When people do speak up, it's worth hearing. In Portswood, residents warned that a bus gate would push traffic onto side streets, and in 2025 the trial was paused for exactly that reason.

Better District makes it easier to speak up. Residents find council plans on a map and can comment, like or post a photo in seconds, so the council hears opinions that would otherwise never reach it. On the council's side, open-source AI running on their own machine summarises the feedback, picks out the changes people are asking for and flags urgent problems. We tested five AI methods on real Southampton consultation comments. None could safely throw comments away, so ours summarises and never deletes.

Councils have paid for consultation tools before. Any council could run this with its own plans.
