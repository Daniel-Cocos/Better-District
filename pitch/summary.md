# Summary

Most people in Southampton notice problems where they live, but few ever tell the council. Having your say usually means a long survey, so most people never do. Yet local knowledge matters: in Portswood, residents warned that a bus gate would push traffic onto side streets, and in 2025 the trial was paused for exactly that reason.

Better District makes speaking up easy. Residents find council plans on a map and comment, like or post a photo in seconds, so the council hears opinions that would otherwise never reach it. Open-source AI on the council's own machine summarises the feedback, picks out the changes people want and flags urgent problems. We tested five AI methods on real Southampton consultation comments; none could safely discard comments, so ours summarises and never deletes.

Councils have paid for consultation tools before, and any council could run this with its own plans.
